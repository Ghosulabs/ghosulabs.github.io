Maybe I can have Claude make me a website that looks just like this kitty terminal, where every post has a title, a date and Ghosu as the author. It should also fix my grammar as much as possible, since mine needs the help. And I need a free or cheap way to host it.

## How this post got here

Written in Obsidian, checked for typos with Harper, then published with one command:

```console
$ newpost "Hello, world (from my terminal)"
$ publish
```

The site is built with Hugo and hosted for free on GitHub Pages. The look is my kitty terminal: Catppuccin Mocha, JetBrains Mono, and `ghosu@blog:~$` at the top.
